A gym, a bathroom scale and a gram food scale are all the plan asks for. Each one sharpens the numbers it gives you.

## Weighing food

Meal targets are written in grams. Weighing food to the single gram means you land on target without eyeballing, which measuring cups and spoons can't match since they drift.

## Bathroom scale

Trend weight steers the plan. Any dependable scale will do, provided you'll stand on it every few days.

- Weigh-in days: 2 a week at the least, 4 or more if you can.
- Weigh when you get up, once you've used the bathroom, in very light clothing.
- Any single reading wanders. Look at the trend.
- Skip the body-fat estimate on a smart scale.

## Gym

Train somewhere with the gear to hit every major muscle, whether that's a gym or your home setup. Dumbbells, a bench, a pull-up bar and a barbell with plates already go a long way. The app doesn't ask about your gear. Choose a template and replace exercises that don't suit what you own.

## Optional

- Weight and steps can come in from Apple Health, which you manage in Settings.
- Bring your phone to the gym so you can log sets.
