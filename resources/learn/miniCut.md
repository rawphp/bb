Think of a mini-cut as a brief, quick fat loss phase lasting 2 to 6 weeks, used to clear away fat that built up while you were gaining muscle.

## Good times for one

- Your body-fat estimate drifted upward while you were gaining muscle
- You'd like to be leaner fast before starting the next gain

If you set up a fat loss phase under 6 weeks long, the app offers a mini-cut in its place.

## Speed

The weekly loss runs from 0.75% to 1% of bodyweight, starting at 1% by default. A normal cut moves slower, and this speed is only acceptable because it doesn't last long.

## Training

In the gym, your goal shifts to protecting hard-won muscle. Keep volume near the minimum effective level, meaning the least work that still drives growth, without pushing it much past that.

## Gain twice as long as you cut

Your gain phases should last at least double your mini-cuts. When the mini-cut runs longer than half of your last muscle gain phase, you'll see a warning, because chaining short cuts together leaves very little building time.

## After it wraps up

The app's usual recommendation afterward is a stretch of maintenance leading into a fresh muscle gain phase. Give your appetite 1 to 4 weeks to settle after any cut ends, and keep cheat foods out until then.

Speak to your doctor before you begin if pregnancy, a medical condition or weight-loss medication applies to you.
