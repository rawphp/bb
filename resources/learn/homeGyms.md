A home gym works well here. The app never asks about your equipment, so you adapt by selecting only exercises you are able to do.

## Picking exercises

Building a mesocycle gives each muscle slot a drop-down of exercises. Choose moves that suit your setup, and filter the library by muscle group and equipment.

The "pick for me" flash button needs a careful eye, because it doesn't know what you own when it fills the empty slots. Check its choices and replace any you can't do.

If your exercise isn't listed, create your own. You give it a name, a muscle, the equipment and a load type.

## Weights and plates

Each week the app raises your load, rounded to what your gear allows. You can tune this.

- Set a load step per exercise, such as 4 kg if that is how your dumbbells climb.
- Own micro plates? Turn them on in Settings and barbell steps are halved.
- When the next jump would be too large, you get an added rep instead.

With light dumbbells, the weight may stay put while the reps climb.

## When your weights run light

Lean on higher rep ranges for muscles your weights can't challenge. Sets of 20 to 30 suit small muscles and anything awkward to load. Lower the weight slowly, hold the stretch for a beat, and take each set close to your reps in reserve target.

## Swapping

Progress is hard to see when exercises keep changing, so have a good reason before you swap. That goes for a one-session swap and for one that lasts the rest of the mesocycle.

## Technique

Open an exercise in the exercise library to see how it is done. That is where the technique videos are.
