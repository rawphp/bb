The last week of every mesocycle is a deload at maintenance calories, so on a cut it doubles as a diet break.

## The reasoning

Deloads are for dropping fatigue. Staying in a deficit keeps adding diet fatigue, which blocks recovery. One of the worst errors is deloading while still dieting. Maintenance calories let the week work.

## During the week

- Every day's calories rise to what that day burns.
- **First half of the week:** sets and reps drop by half, and the weight stays where it was.
- **Second half:** the weight is cut in half as well.
- It ought to feel easy. Before it starts, push your last hard week right to 0 RIR, or as near to it as you can get.
- Look for "Deload · maintenance calories" on the schedule.

## Cuts specifically

- Long cuts place deloads every 4 to 6 weeks.
- A fat-loss phase longer than 8 weeks includes at least one.
- Neither your weekly rate nor the 16-week limit counts diet-break weeks.
- One deload week can skip maintenance calories if you choose, after a warning from the app.

## Scale blip

Expect your weight to rise slightly. Extra carbs and food hold water, which explains most of it. Your weekly review factors it in and keeps calories steady instead of reducing them.

## Other phases

Gain phases deload at maintenance calories too. Because they're scheduled, your end date doesn't shift.
